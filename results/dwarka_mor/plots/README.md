## convergence.png — What to Expect

The convergence plot shows reward over training rounds/updates:

```
Reward
  ^
  |  AdaptFlow  --------_______________  (stable high reward)
  |  MA2C      ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~     (noisy but improving)
  |  FedDQN    ---___________________   (slow improvement)
  +----------------------------------------> Rounds / Steps
```

- **AdaptFlow** should show more stable convergence due to adaptive clustering
- **MA2C** will show noisy improvement (on-policy variance)
- **FedDQN** starts lower (high epsilon) then improves as epsilon decays

## radar.png — Multi-Metric Spider Chart

The radar chart plots 5 axes (normalized 0–1, higher = better for all):

| Axis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| TP Ratio | Throughput — fraction of vehicles cleared |
| Inv Wait | 1 - normalized_wait — lower wait = higher on chart |
| Inv Queue | 1 - normalized_queue — lower queue = higher on chart |
| Reward | Normalized final reward |
| Stability | 1 - coefficient_of_variation of reward — less noisy = higher |

A larger radar area = better overall algorithm performance.
AdaptFlow should have the largest covered area if training was successful.
